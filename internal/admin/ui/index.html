New job

A job is a schedule plus the name of a handler. Nothing runs it until an executor registers that handler key.

Identity
Unique within the tenant. Names the job in every log line, audit entry and execution key.
Must match a key an executor declares, exactly. No match means the job materializes and is never dispatched.
Schedule
CRON — one execution per fire instant, so a missed one is still a missed occurrence. FIXED_DELAY — one pass at a time, the next scheduled a fixed delay after the last finishes.
Six fields, seconds first: sec min hour dom month dow. Evaluated in the deployment's business time zone. For FIXED_DELAY, the delay in seconds.
What happens to instants missed while nothing was running. FIRE_ONCE catches up with a single run. SKIP advances to the next future instant and runs nothing — a five-minute outage costs a daily job its whole day.
Disabled keeps the configuration and the schedule but is never claimed. Use it to stage a job before cutting over to it.
Routing and concurrency
Empty means any executor serving the handler. Naming a group confines the job to it — how a canary takes a slice of traffic without taking production's.
QUEUE — a due occurrence waits for the running one. FORBID — it is skipped outright and recorded as skipped.
Limits
Runtime cap for one attempt, started at the claim. Passing it is terminal and is not retried — a run that used its whole budget will most likely use it again.
How long this scheduler may go without renewing before another may recover the work. Minimum 10, and must not exceed the timeout. Leave it alone unless you know why.
Tries per occurrence, including the first. A failure whose failure_kind starts with permanent stops early instead of burning the budget.
A separate budget for attempts lost to a crashed scheduler or an unreachable executor — where nobody knows whether the work ran. Spending it does not consume an attempt.
Payload
A JSON object handed to the handler. A manual trigger's overrides are merged over these, not substituted. No secrets — these are stored in the tenant schema, shown here, and sent over the wire.
